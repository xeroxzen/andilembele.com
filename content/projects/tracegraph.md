## Problem

When something goes wrong at a regulated institution, the evidence of what happened is already in its audit logs. TraceGraph rebuilds the incident from those logs instead of asking teams to collect it again.

> [!IMPORTANT] Evidence that verifies offline
> Every step in a reconstructed incident links back to the original record in a signed ledger, so a reviewer can check the chain without access to the source systems.
